About the Conference Series

The International Conference on Nonlinear Applied Analysis and Optimization (ICNAAO) brings together leading academic scientists, researchers, and research scholars to exchange and share their experiences and research results on all aspects of nonlinear analysis and optimization.

It also provides a premier interdisciplinary platform for researchers, practitioners, and educators to present and discuss the most recent innovations, trends, and concerns as well as practical challenges encountered and solutions adopted in the fields of Mathematics and Computation.

History of ICNAAO

The International Conference on Nonlinear Applied Analysis and Optimization (ICNAAO) was initiated in 2021 and has since developed into a recurring international conference series. Over the years, the conference has been organized by several leading institutions and has brought together distinguished researchers from universities and research institutions around the world.

ICNAAO 2026

ICNAAO 2026 continues the conference series in Bangkok, Thailand, hosted by the Department of Mathematics, King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi (KMUTT). The conference continues the tradition of bringing together researchers working in nonlinear analysis, optimization, variational analysis, computation, and related applications.

About KMUTT

King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi (KMUTT) is renowned for its excellence in science, technology, and innovation in Thailand. Located in Bangkok, KMUTT fosters a vibrant ecosystem for international research collaborations, advanced engineering, and rigorous mathematical sciences.

The Department of Mathematics at KMUTT has a strong tradition of promoting high-level research in optimization theory, operational research, fixed-point theory, and applied functional analysis.

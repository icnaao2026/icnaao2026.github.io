Dear \SpeakerSalutation,

On behalf of the Organizing Committee, I am pleased to formally invite you
to serve as a \SpeakerRole{} Speaker at the \textbf{6th International Conference
on Nonlinear Applied Analysis and Optimization (ICNAAO 2026)}, to be held
on \textbf{December 14--16, 2026}, at \textbf{King Mongkut's University of
Technology Thonburi (KMUTT), Bangkok, Thailand}.

The conference is organized by the Department of Mathematics, KMUTT, in
collaboration with the Nonlinear Analysis and Optimization (NAO) Wing of
the Calcutta Mathematical Society (CMS), Kolkata. It will bring together
researchers and students to exchange ideas and discuss recent advances
in nonlinear analysis, applied mathematics, optimization, and their applications.

In recognition of your contributions to \ResearchArea, we would be honored
to welcome you and hear your lecture on a topic of your choice within the
scope of the conference. Your participation would be a valuable contribution
to the scientific program.

We would be grateful if you could confirm your acceptance and availability.
Upon acceptance, please provide a tentative lecture
title and any scheduling preferences. We will follow up regarding your

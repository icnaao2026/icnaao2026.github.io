# ICNAAO 2026: Proposed Section Responsibilities

**Conference:** 14–16 December 2026, KMUTT, Bangkok, Thailand  
**Status:** Proposal for committee discussion and agreement  
**Thai version:** [README.th.md](README.th.md)

This proposal assigns responsibilities before and during the conference based on the committee structure, tentative program, and registration information in the repository. Each section head is accountable for planning, assigning staff, monitoring readiness, and resolving issues within their section. Each should appoint a deputy to handle routine decisions in their absence.

## 1. Academic Section

**Head: Pawaton Kaemawichanurat**

**Role:** Ensure academic quality and prepare and deliver a coherent scientific program.

### Before the conference

- Manage abstract submissions, check completeness and relevance, coordinate reviewers, and prepare acceptance decisions under criteria agreed with the Conference Chair.
- Prepare acceptance letters and presentation instructions; request corrected abstracts when necessary.
- Define slide submission requirements: accepted file formats, slide aspect ratio, file naming, submission channel, submission window, and deadline with an explicit time zone. Coordinate technical requirements with Reception and Venue and provide confirmed instructions to PR for distribution.
- Track slide submissions, acknowledge receipt, follow up on missing files, and define a cutoff and procedure for replacement files. Organize the latest confirmed files by room and session and hand them over to room assistants and technical staff before presentations.
- Coordinate keynote and invited speakers with the Conference Chair and obtain confirmations, biographies, and presentation requirements.
- Group talks by topic, allocate presentation and discussion time, avoid speaker conflicts, and appoint session chairs. Plan separate chairing and support for each of the two parallel sessions.
- Confirm a chairperson for every session and prepare a duty roster with backup personnel. Provide the program, speaker names and biographies, introduction guidance, and procedures for discussion and time control.
- Assign room assistants and timekeepers for every lecture room and session, with clear duties and shifts. Academic manages these assignments and briefings; Reception and Venue supports volunteer recruitment and technical assistance.
- Prepare and distribute timing cards for each room, such as “5 minutes remaining,” “1 minute remaining,” and “Time is up,” with a timer and backup. Agree on warning intervals appropriate to each presentation length and brief chairpersons, speakers, and timekeepers on the signals.
- Prepare room assistant checklists covering speaker check-in, presentation file readiness, microphone support during questions, presentation records, and contact procedures for technical problems.
- Compile and proofread the abstract book and detailed program, verifying names, affiliations, titles, and session assignments.
- Reconcile the presenter list with Registration before finalizing the program. Refer unresolved registration cases for a decision under the agreed policy.
- Prepare procedures for absent or late speakers, overrunning talks, and technical interruptions.
- Confirm special issue arrangements and communicate submission procedures accurately. Conference acceptance does not imply journal acceptance, which remains subject to the journal's review process.

### On conference days

- Supervise scientific sessions and brief session chairs on timing, introductions, discussion, and attendance reporting.
- Check that each room has its chairperson, assistants, timekeeper, timing cards, and timer before the session starts; arrange replacements when needed.
- Time each presentation and discussion, display the agreed warning cards, and alert the chairperson when time expires so the session stays on schedule.
- Have room assistants confirm the next speaker, coordinate presentation file readiness with technical staff, assist with microphones during questions, and record actual presentations and any problems.
- Confirm speaker presence before sessions and resolve academic schedule changes.
- Coordinate equipment problems and room readiness with Reception and Venue.
- Provide approved program corrections to PR for immediate communication.
- Record actual presentations, withdrawals, and absent speakers; verify presentation certificate eligibility.

**Principal deliverables:** Reviewed submission list, confirmed speaker list, detailed scientific program, abstract book, chairperson and room assistant duty roster with backups, session chair briefing, room assistant checklists, timing cards and timers for every room, and presentation records.

## 2. Registration and Financial Section

**Head: Sukuman Sarikavanij**

**Role:** Maintain accurate participant and financial records and provide an orderly registration and payment service.

### Before the conference

- Maintain one authoritative participant register, including category, affiliation, contact details, accompanying persons, and relevant attendance requirements.
- Complete and test payment arrangements, communicate instructions, verify payments, and prepare invoices and receipts through the appropriate university process.
- Apply approved fee policies, check student eligibility and early registration rates, and refer exceptional discounts, refunds, or complimentary attendance to the Conference Chair.
- Consolidate budget estimates from all sections, track commitments and income, identify funding gaps, and arrange approved purchasing and reimbursement procedures.
- Prepare badge data, check-in lists, receipt records, and certificate name data. Coordinate conference kit quantities and assembly with Reception and Venue.
- Verify participant details and registration status for invitation letters; prepare letters for authorized signature and coordinate academic details with Academic.
- Supply updated meal and banquet counts, accompanying person numbers, and dietary or accessibility requirements to Reception and Venue, sharing only necessary information.

### On conference days

- Operate the registration desk, including check-in, badge corrections, kit distribution, and late registration under agreed rules.
- Resolve payment discrepancies discreetly and issue receipts through the approved process.
- Maintain attendance and payment records and notify Academic of presenter registration issues.
- Control on-site payments and petty cash, if used, with documented transactions and daily reconciliation.
- Verify certificate names and eligibility and coordinate distribution with the other sections.
- Track approved additional expenses and retain supporting documents.

**Principal deliverables:** Participant register, payment reconciliation, budget tracker, receipts, registration materials, and verified certificate data.

## 3. Reception and Venue Section

**Head: Songpon Sriwongsa**

**Role:** Welcome participants and ensure that the venue, equipment, hospitality, and physical arrangements support the program.

### Before the conference

- Confirm plenary and parallel session rooms, registration and dining areas, capacity, accessibility, and opening hours.
- Test projectors, microphones, presentation computers, connections, internet access, and backup equipment; assign a technical contact for each session room.
- Confirm supported slide formats, software, fonts, and embedded media with Academic; test submitted files on the presentation computers and prepare offline backups. Report problems to Academic for follow-up with presenters.
- Arrange coffee breaks, lunches, drinking water, and the banquet using confirmed participant counts and the approved budget.
- Coordinate speaker arrival assistance, accommodation liaison, and transport where the committee has agreed to provide them.
- Liaise with hotels and accommodation near KMUTT's Bangmod campus to confirm room availability during the conference, room rates or participant discounts, booking and cancellation terms, and access to the venue. Provide confirmed information to PR for participants. Obtain approval before committing to room blocks or expenses on behalf of the organizers.
- Prepare room layouts, sign locations, registration furniture, kit assembly, and installation schedules.
- Recruit and brief volunteers for reception, room support, participant guidance, and hospitality, with clear shifts and reporting lines.
- Establish contacts and procedures for equipment failure, transport delays, lost property, medical assistance, and evacuation through university services.
- Prepare seating, microphones, stage arrangements, and rehearsals for the opening and closing ceremonies.

### On conference days

- Inspect rooms and equipment before participants arrive and keep technical support available throughout sessions.
- Welcome and guide participants and assist invited guests.
- Deliver refreshments, lunches, and banquet arrangements on schedule.
- Supervise volunteers, room setup, cleanliness, accessibility, and replenishment of supplies.
- Respond to facility and welfare issues and escalate urgent incidents through the appropriate university services.
- Implement approved room changes and inform Academic and PR immediately.

**Principal deliverables:** Venue plan, equipment checklist, hospitality arrangements, volunteer roster, guest reception plan, and operational contact list.

## 4. PR Section

**Head: Pirun Dilokpatpongsa**

**Role:** Ensure that conference information is accurate, consistent, timely, and accessible to prospective and registered participants.

### Before the conference

- Coordinate promotion through academic networks, university channels, partner institutions, and suitable social media.
- Maintain the website, announcements, and participant guidance using information confirmed by the responsible section.
- Design the program layout, badges, certificates, banners, signs, and other materials. Academic verifies scientific content, Registration verifies participant data, and Reception confirms placement and quantities.
- Acknowledge and route general inquiries to the responsible section and track unresolved requests.
- Prepare deadline reminders, registration instructions, arrival information, venue maps, and final practical guidance.
- Publish Academic's confirmed slide submission requirements, submission window, deadline and time zone, submission channel, and replacement-file procedure; send reminders to presenters before the deadline.
- Arrange photography, university media coverage, approved acknowledgments, and any recording plans with appropriate speaker agreement.

### On conference days

- Publish confirmed schedule and room changes promptly through the agreed channels.
- Keep digital information current and coordinate corrected signs with Reception and Venue.
- Support announcements and ceremony scripts with the designated master of ceremonies.
- Coordinate photography, group photographs, and conference updates without disrupting sessions.
- Route participant questions and media requests to the responsible person.
- Refer sensitive statements or incident communications to the Conference Chair.

**Principal deliverables:** Communication calendar, current website, participant information pack, approved designs, announcement process, and conference photographs.

## 5. Coordination and decision authority

| Matter | Accountable section | Required coordination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Abstract acceptance and scientific schedule | Academic | Chair decides exceptional cases; PR publishes confirmed information. |
| Slide submission requirements, deadlines, and file handover | Academic | Reception and Venue checks compatibility and prepares presentation computers; PR communicates instructions and reminders. |
| Registration status, payments, and receipts | Registration and Financial | Academic checks presenter readiness. |
| Rooms, equipment, catering, and guest logistics | Reception and Venue | Academic supplies requirements; Registration supplies counts and budget checks. |
| Public announcements and printed designs | PR | Responsible section verifies facts before release. |
| Certificates | Registration verifies identity and attendance | Academic verifies presentations; PR prepares the design. |
| Opening and closing ceremonies | Reception and Venue coordinates delivery | Chair approves proceedings; Academic and PR prepare relevant content. |

The Conference Chair retains approval of the overall budget, exceptional fee arrangements, major program changes, institutional commitments, and sensitive external communications. Section heads should handle routine matters within the approved plan and delegated budget authority.

- Use one shared action list with an owner and deadline for every task.
- Hold weekly coordination meetings during preparation and a brief operational meeting each conference morning.
- Maintain a shared contact list and one agreed channel for urgent operational updates.

## 6. Items requiring confirmation

The repository currently describes the payment link as under preparation. Registration and Financial should complete and verify this process, then provide confirmed instructions to PR.

Registration includes lunches on all three days and a banquet, while the tentative program ends at 12:30 on the final day and does not yet show the banquet. Registration and Reception should confirm these arrangements, Academic should incorporate the timing, and PR should update the published information.

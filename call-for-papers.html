Scope and Topics

Original research contributions falling within the scope of nonlinear analysis and mathematical optimization are cordially invited. Suggested areas include, but are not limited to:

  • Nonlinear Functional Analysis
  • Fixed Point Theory
  • Differential Equations and Dynamical Systems
  • Convex and Non-Convex Optimization
  • Stochastic Optimization and Uncertainty Quantification
  • Numerical Optimization and Algorithms
  • Integer and Combinatorial Linear Programming
  • Variational Inequalities, Complementarity Problems and Equilibrium Problems
  • Game Theory and Nash Equilibrium Problems
  • Decision Science and Fuzzy Logic
  • Computational Science, Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence
  • Applications in Operations Research, Economics, and Engineering Systems
  • Miscellaneous Topics in Mathematics

Submission Guidelines

Prospective authors are invited to submit an abstract outlining their original research work via the official conference submission portal or by email to the organizing committee.
